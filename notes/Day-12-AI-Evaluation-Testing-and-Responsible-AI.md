# Day 12 – AI Evaluation, Testing and Responsible AI

## 1. Introduction
AI Evaluation is the process of measuring how well an AI system performs its tasks.

AI Testing checks whether the application works correctly under different conditions.

## 2. Why Is AI Evaluation Important?
- Identifies incorrect outputs.
- Measures system performance.
- Helps improve response quality.
- Checks whether AI follows instructions.
- Improves reliability.

## 3. Important Evaluation Metrics

- **Accuracy:** How often results are correct.
- **Precision:** How many identified results are actually correct.
- **Recall:** How many relevant results were successfully identified.
- **Relevance:** How useful the response is.
- **Latency:** How long the system takes to respond.

## 4. AI Testing
AI systems should be tested with different inputs and situations.

Examples:
- Valid input.
- Invalid input.
- Missing information.
- Empty documents.
- Unexpected questions.

## 5. Responsible AI
Responsible AI focuses on designing and using AI safely, fairly and transparently.

Important principles:
- Fairness.
- Privacy.
- Transparency.
- Safety.
- Human oversight.
- Accountability.

## 6. Application in CareerPilot AI
CareerPilot AI should:
- Protect uploaded resumes.
- Avoid inventing qualifications.
- Distinguish confirmed skills from inferred skills.
- Explain its recommendations.
- Allow users to correct inaccurate information.
- Avoid guaranteeing employment outcomes.

## 7. Key Takeaways
- Evaluation measures AI performance.
- Testing identifies errors and unexpected behavior.
- Precision and recall measure different aspects of performance.
- Responsible AI considers fairness, privacy and safety.
- Human oversight is important for significant decisions.
